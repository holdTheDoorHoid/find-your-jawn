Hi Brian,

I read the Billy Penn piece about Join Philly and love what you're building. I'm working on a free,
open source project called Find Your Jawn that comes at the same problem from a different side: an
exhaustive directory of Philly community groups (civic associations, volunteer nonprofits, student
groups, town watches, Friends of park groups, parade clubs, faith service programs, hobby clubs) plus
a short quiz that recommends a few groups with a reason for each and a concrete first step, including
a couple of gentle "stretch" picks to nudge people out of their comfort zone.

I think we complement each other. You curate social clubs and bring people together in person; we
aim for breadth, matching and follow through. A few ideas, none of which I'd do without your okay:

1. Link to Join Philly from every club listing you also carry, and credit you on our About page.
2. Let our quiz send people to your listings and activities fairs.
3. Share what we learn: our directory data will be openly licensed, so if it helps you find clubs
   for Join Philly, it's yours to use.
