# Ethics (binding)

Rules about people, privacy, scope and fairness. Owner decisions from 2026-10-04 are marked (owner).

## People and contact details

- (owner) Contact details are copied as the group or a public source published them, including an
  organizer's personal phone or email. Every contact shows the source it came from and the date.
- (owner) Corrections and removal requests come through GitHub issue forms only. A link to the
  removal form is on every group page. Removal requests are honored promptly and the details are
  marked so harvests do not bring them back (`hidden_reason: removal_request` or a field level block
  list in `data/blocklist.yaml`).
- Never publish anything about a person beyond their role in a group: no home addresses that are not
  the group's published meeting place, no birthdays, no photos of individuals, no social profiles of
  individuals unless the group published them as its contact.
- Never contact groups or people. Outreach drafts go to `docs/outreach/` for the owner to send.

## Scope and fairness

- (owner) Partisan political groups are out. Nonpartisan civic, voter education and advocacy groups
  are in, including groups whose causes some people oppose.
- (owner) Faith communities are in, clearly labeled, with an include, exclude or "only my tradition"
  choice. Never a stretch for someone who excluded faith.
- (owner) Support and recovery groups appear only when someone asks for support, never in general
  results and never as a stretch. The support flow shows crisis lines first.
- Hate groups, groups that promote violence, and pyramid schemes are excluded. Affinity groups for a
  community (women only, LGBTQ, a heritage, a faith, veterans) are welcome.
- No paid placement, no sponsored ranking, ever. How ranking works is published in plain words.
- Court ordered service and background checks are handled without judgment. The quiz never asks about
  a record; it asks whether the person prefers roles without background checks.

## Accuracy

- Facts, not text: summaries are written in our own words. Never republish another directory's or a
  group's descriptions. Short factual items (name, schedule, cost, address) are fine with a source.
- Every listing shows "last seen active" and its research tier. Unverified groups are labeled.
- `court_ordered_ok: yes` and similar claims need a public source. Never infer them.
- When we cannot verify something, say "unknown", never guess.

## Sources and partners

- Respect site terms, robots rules, logins and blocks. Platforms whose terms forbid scraping or
  republishing are leads only.
- (owner) Join Philly's list is used only as leads; we verify each group elsewhere, never copy their
  text, and credit and link them on the About page.
- Credit every data source on the About page with its required attribution.

## Visitors

- No accounts, no cookies, no tracking at launch. Quiz answers stay in the visitor's browser.
- Optional identity questions (communities, faith, age) are framed as "people you'd like to find",
  never required, never sent anywhere.
- Language is plain and welcoming to every background, reading level, and ability.
