# Wave w6-tier2-deep-a, first visit guides (2026-10-08, nightly runner)

The first tier 2 wave. The brief (`research/briefs/tier2.md`) was written in this run from
docs/RESEARCH.md section 7. The 56 groups were chosen by a small script: active, open to the public,
tier 1, with a website, highest newcomer rating first, two or three from each of the 26 interest
families. The list is in `research/batches/w6-tier2-deep-a-ids.json`.

## How it ran

One pilot agent went first on batch 01. Its records were imported alone and the diffs read: tier 1
fields were carried over, nothing was wiped, and the first visit details were specific and sourced.
Two rules were added to the brief after the pilot: no one time promotions in schedule or cost text,
and leave `access.notes` out instead of writing "not stated". The pilot's seven records keep a few
"accessibility is not stated" notes; harmless, but worth tidying on a later pass. Then the other seven
agents ran, four at a time. 3 searches used in all (agents worked from the groups' own sites).

## Results

- 56 records in, all merged, none held. All verdict publish.
- Newcomer ratings: 5 for 2 groups, 4 for 33, 3 for 20, 2 for 1.
- `fyj check`: 1183 groups pass, 0 held back. Pipeline tests 364 passed, site tests 518 passed, site
  build 1227 pages.
- Usage: the weekly meter went from 12 to 15 percent for this wave, about four times a tier 1 group
  each, in line with the plan's estimate.
