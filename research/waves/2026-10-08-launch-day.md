Published after launch day: 787 groups in all 18 planning districts and 28 interest families.

What we learned:

- Chapter finders are mostly search forms or bot checked, so lane P yields 4 to 23 groups per agent.
  The interest sweeps now start from roundup and list pages, which name many groups per search.
- Haiku agents stopped early with unchecked leads that had working websites; the shared rules now
  require every such lead to be checked before finishing.
- Haiku agents sometimes called unrelated tools (a stray suggestion card, once a stray agent). A
  restricted `fyj-researcher` agent type now exists for new sessions.
- Agents often leave out `audience.open_to` when a group simply invites people to come; the
  importer now defaults it to public (students for student groups) and records the repair.
- Open question for the owner: bar and brewery quiz nights (about 100 weekly ones in a Billy Penn
  guide). Not listed for now.
- Spot check (3 random new records: a Community Schools site, Play On Philly, Penn AC Rowing):
  all real, statuses supported, summaries fair and in our own words.
